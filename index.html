About me

Curious about the choices
behind complex systems.

I am an undergraduate in Data Science and Big Data Technology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

My research focuses on language-model agents: how they use interaction history and persistent memory, adapt their strategies, and respond to market rules. I have worked on coupled electricity-carbon markets and currently study pricing agents under changing demand. I am also interested in supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for reliable structured output.

I also lead DWTS Voting Analysis, extending my team’s 2026 MCM study into public research software and follow-up experiments.
