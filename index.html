About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I work on large language model agents in strategic environments, where an agent’s payoff depends on what other agents do, and the question is not whether a model answers correctly but how its behaviour changes as it accumulates experience.

My research so far has applied this to coupled electricity–carbon markets, and my current project moves to repeated pricing markets under changing demand. It centers around three topics:

  • LLM Agents and Multi-Agent Systems: How language-model agents form, retain and revise strategies through repeated interaction, and what interaction history and persistent memory contribute to that process.
  • Strategic Behaviour in Markets: Bidding in coupled electricity–carbon markets, algorithmic pricing, tacit collusion, and the oversight mechanisms meant to detect and constrain it.
  • LLM Post-Training: Supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for reliable structured output.
