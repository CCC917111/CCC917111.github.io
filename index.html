First author · IEEE PES APPEEC 2026

Strategic
bidding

A generation company has to account for electricity and carbon costs. We separate cost calculation from strategic markup, then use rule checks and recent market feedback to guide a language-model bidder.

On the IEEE 14-bus system, it earned higher average profit than a fixed-markup baseline while clearing in every round, without fine-tuning.

DWTS voting
analysis

What can public scores and eliminations reveal when audience votes stay private? I lead a project combining Bayesian support models, voting-rule comparisons and historical elimination forecasts, building on our MCM team study.

Under a declared deterministic voting rule, many vote allocations can explain the same exit. Comparing alternative rules therefore requires explicit assumptions about the hidden votes.
